# /network [company | jobs/<slug> | linkedin-about]

## Rules
- **Never send anything.** Every message is a draft the candidate copies. Never log into LinkedIn or act on their account; use the Browser pane / WebSearch only to read public pages.
- Don't scrape or compile personal data about individuals beyond their public role/title. Name roles ("BCG X Perth recruiter", "alumni of your current employer now at BCG") and let the candidate find the person.

## For a company / role
1. Read `jobs/<slug>/jd.md` (if any), `profile/career-context.md`, `profile/master-resume.md`.
2. **Warm paths first:** your university's alumni at the company, alumni of your current employer, society/volunteering contacts, people who engaged with their LinkedIn posts, the recruiter named in the JD (BCG ANZ: recruitmentinANZ@bcg.com).
3. **Target list** (5–8 role types, not names): why each, what to ask, what the candidate offers in return.
4. **Drafts** (each ≤ 300 characters for LinkedIn connection notes, ≤ 120 words for InMail/email): connection request, follow-up after accept, referral ask (only after a conversation), thank-you. Specific, no flattery, one clear ask (a 15-minute chat about X). Include a "why me" line grounded in master.
5. **Events/communities:** Perth and online (BCG X info sessions late Feb — check careers.bcg.com/events; Oracle user groups; AI meetups; Anthropic community/Discord; your university's alumni events). Verify dates with WebSearch; say "verify" if you can't.
6. **Cadence:** a 2-week plan (3 outreach/day max, follow-up after 5 business days, stop after 2 follow-ups).
Write to `jobs/<slug>/network.md` or `reports/network-<company>-<today>.md`.

## `linkedin-about`
Using `profile/linkedin-audit.md` and master: write the new headline (≤ 220 chars), About (≤ 2,000 chars, 4 short paragraphs: what they does now / what they builds / credentials / what they's looking for + open to relocation), the your current employer experience description (5 bullets, clients anonymised), the 40-skill list in priority order, 3 Featured items, and Open-to-Work settings. Australian English, first person for About only. Output as `profile/linkedin-rewrite.md` for the candidate to paste.
